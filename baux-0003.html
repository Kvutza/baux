Local Epistemic Geometry & Coherent Draws

1. Precision-Weighted Neighborhood Moments & Pseudolikelihood

Let \(\mathcal {X} \subseteq \mathbb {R}^D\) denote solver coordinates and \(\mathcal {D} = \{(x_m, y_m, s_m)\}_{m=1}^N \subset \mathcal {X} \times \mathbb {R} \times \mathbb {R}_+\) denote \(N\) heteroscedastic observations \(y_m = f(x_m) + s_m \varepsilon _m\) with \(\varepsilon _m \stackrel {\text {i.i.d.}}{\sim } \mathcal {N}(0, 1)\). Given a nonnegative pairwise dissimilarity \(\delta : \mathcal {X} \times \mathcal {X} \to \mathbb {R}_+\), empirical target variance scale \(\hat {\sigma }_y^2 > 0\), and dimensionless scale parameters \(\theta = (s_0^2, c_e) \in \mathbb {R}_+^2\), Epistemic Nearest Neighbors (Bafna et al. [BJS25]) assigns datum \(m\) at query \(x\) the working moments

\[ \mu (x \mid x_m) = y_m, \qquad \sigma _a^2(x \mid x_m) = \hat {\sigma }_y^2 s_0^2 + s_m^2, \qquad \sigma _e^2(x \mid x_m) = \hat {\sigma }_y^2 c_e\, \delta (x, x_m). \]

Writing \(N_K(x) \eqdef \{\pi _1(x), \dots , \pi _K(x)\}\) for the \(K\) nearest indices ordered by nondecreasing \(\delta (x, x_{\pi _i(x)})\) (ties broken by datum index) and precision weights \(w_i(x) \eqdef \bigl (\sigma _a^2(x \mid x_{\pi _i(x)}) + \sigma _e^2(x \mid x_{\pi _i(x)})\bigr )^{-1}\) on non-degenerate neighborhoods, the local predictive moments decompose epistemic and aleatoric dispersion in \(\mathcal {O}(N D + N \log K)\) exact scan time per query:

\[ \mu (x) = \frac {\sum _{i=1}^K w_i(x)\, y_{\pi _i(x)}}{\sum _{i=1}^K w_i(x)}, \qquad \sigma _a^2(x) = \frac {\sum _{i=1}^K w_i(x)\, \sigma _a^2(x \mid x_{\pi _i(x)})}{\sum _{i=1}^K w_i(x)}, \qquad \sigma _e^2(x) = \frac {1}{\sum _{i=1}^K w_i(x)}, \]

with total predictive variance \(\sigma ^2(x) \eqdef \sigma _e^2(x) + \sigma _a^2(x)\). Scale parameters \(\theta = (s_0^2, c_e)\) maximize a subsampled leave-one-out Gaussian log-pseudolikelihood over \(S_P \subset \{1, \dots , N\}\) of size \(P \le N\) [BJS25]:

\[ \widehat {\log \mathcal {L}}(\theta ) \eqdef -\frac {1}{2P} \sum _{n \in S_P} \left [\log \bigl (2\pi \sigma _{-n}^2(x_n; \theta )\bigr ) + \frac {\bigl (y_n - \mu _{-n}(x_n; \theta )\bigr )^2}{\sigma _{-n}^2(x_n; \theta )}\right ], \]

where \(\mu _{-n}\) and \(\sigma _{-n}^2\) exclude datum \(n\) from its own \(K\)-neighbor set.

2. Coherent Function Draws Across Order-\(K\) Voronoi Cells

Posterior sampling over candidate pools requires spatially correlated draws without factoring a \(Q \times Q\) joint query covariance. Tying standard normal perturbations to global datum indices induces positive spatial covariance wherever order-\(K\) Voronoi neighborhoods overlap:

Proposition 1.1 (Coherent Function Draws). Fix draw index \(s\) and output channel \(j\), and attach \(z_{s,m,j} \stackrel {\text {i.i.d.}}{\sim } \mathcal {N}(0, 1)\) to each datum \(m \in \{1, \dots , N\}\). Normalize the active precision weights and extend them by zero outside \(N_K(x)\) by setting \(\tilde {w}_{m,j}(x) \eqdef \bar {w}_{i,j}(x)\) if \(m = \pi _i(x) \in N_K(x)\) and \(0\) otherwise, where

\[ \bar {w}_{i,j}(x) \eqdef \frac {w_{i,j}(x)}{\sum _{r=1}^K w_{r,j}(x)}, \qquad \Vert \bar {w}_j(x)\Vert _2 \eqdef \sqrt {\sum _{i=1}^K \bar {w}_{i,j}^2(x)}, \qquad \tilde {f}_{s,j}(x) \eqdef \mu _j(x) + \frac {\sigma _{e,j}(x)}{\Vert \bar {w}_j(x)\Vert _2} \sum _{i=1}^K \bar {w}_{i,j}(x)\, z_{s,\pi _i(x),j}. \]

Then for every \(x \in \mathcal {X}\), \(\tilde {f}_{s,j}(x) \sim \mathcal {N}\bigl (\mu _j(x),\, \sigma _{e,j}^2(x)\bigr )\), sample paths are smooth on the interior of each order-\(K\) Voronoi cell where \(N_K(x)\) is constant, and any \(x, x' \in \mathcal {X}\) with nonempty neighborhood intersection \(S_K(x, x') \eqdef N_K(x) \cap N_K(x') \ne \emptyset \) satisfy

\[ \operatorname {Cov}\bigl (\tilde {f}_{s,j}(x),\, \tilde {f}_{s,j}(x')\bigr ) = \frac {\sigma _{e,j}(x)\,\sigma _{e,j}(x')}{\Vert \bar {w}_j(x)\Vert _2\,\Vert \bar {w}_j(x')\Vert _2} \sum _{m \in S_K(x, x')} \tilde {w}_{m,j}(x)\,\tilde {w}_{m,j}(x') > 0. \]

Proof. Distinctness of \(\pi _1(x), \dots , \pi _K(x)\) implies that \(\xi _j(x) \eqdef \sum _{m=1}^N \tilde {w}_{m,j}(x)\, z_{s,m,j}\) is zero-mean Gaussian with variance \(\sum _{i=1}^K \bar {w}_{i,j}^2(x) = \Vert \bar {w}_j(x)\Vert _2^2 > 0\); rescaling by \(\tfrac {\sigma _{e,j}(x)}{\Vert \bar {w}_j(x)\Vert _2}\) yields \(\tilde {f}_{s,j}(x) \sim \mathcal {N}(\mu _j(x), \sigma _{e,j}^2(x))\). For any \(x, x'\), \(\mathbb {E}[z_{s,m,j}\, z_{s,m',j}] = \delta _{m,m'}\) gives \(\operatorname {Cov}(\xi _j(x), \xi _j(x')) = \sum _{m \in S_K(x, x')} \tilde {w}_{m,j}(x)\,\tilde {w}_{m,j}(x')\), which is strictly positive whenever \(S_K(x, x') \ne \emptyset \) since every active precision weight is strictly positive.\(\;\square \)

3. Non-Stationary Local-Scale Dissimilarities

Sequential optimization concentrates evaluations exponentially inside active trust-region basins while leaving the global domain sparsely sampled. Let \(d(x, x') \eqdef \Vert x - x'\Vert _2\) denote Euclidean (or coordinate-scaled) distance and let \(\sigma (x) > 0\) denote the \(k_0\)-th positive Euclidean neighbor radius at \(x\) relative to \(\mathcal {D}\). Because \(\sigma (x)\) depends on the empirical configuration, rescales of \(d\) define symmetric nonnegative dissimilarities tailored to multi-scale sampling density:

Conformal normalization (Zelnik-Manor & Perona [ZP04]) rescales squared Euclidean separation by the geometric-mean endpoint scale:

\[ \delta _c(x, x_m) \eqdef \frac {d^2(x, x_m)}{\sigma (x)\,\sigma (x_m)}. \]

Poincaré half-space geometry (Cannon et al. [CFK+97], Nickel & Kiela [NK17], Kisfaludi-Bak & van Wordragen [KvW24]) lifts each point into the upper half-space \(\mathbb {H}(\mathcal {X}) \eqdef \mathcal {X} \times \mathbb {R}_+\) equipped with \(ds^2 = \tfrac {\Vert dx\Vert _2^2 + d\sigma ^2}{\sigma ^2}\) via \(\Lambda (x) \eqdef (x, \sigma (x))\):

\[ \begin {CD} \mathcal {X} \times \mathcal {X} @>{\Lambda \times \Lambda }>> \mathbb {H}(\mathcal {X}) \times \mathbb {H}(\mathcal {X}) \\ @V{\delta _h}VV @VV{\cosh d_{\mathbb {H}} - 1}V \\ \mathbb {R}_+ @= \mathbb {R}_+ \end {CD} \]

Evaluating the isometric chordal invariant \(\cosh d_{\mathbb {H}}\bigl (\Lambda (x), \Lambda (x_m)\bigr ) - 1\) adds a dimensionless vertical scale-mismatch barrier to conformal horizontal distance:

\[ \delta _h(x, x_m) \eqdef \frac {d^2(x, x_m)}{2\,\sigma (x)\,\sigma (x_m)} + \frac {\bigl (\sigma (x) - \sigma (x_m)\bigr )^2}{2\,\sigma (x)\,\sigma (x_m)}. \]

When a trust region collapses onto an incumbent basin (\(\sigma (x) \ll \sigma (x_m)\)), the vertical term scales as \(\mathcal {O}\Bigl (\tfrac {\sigma (x_m)}{\sigma (x)}\Bigr )\), quarantining local neighborhood estimates from coarse outer observations even at moderate Euclidean proximity, while symmetrically preventing exploratory queries in sparse regions from collapsing onto dense clusters. Replacing hyperbolic normalization with an additive 1D Bures–Wasserstein radius cost motivated by Gaussian optimal transport (Villani [Vil09], Peyré & Cuturi [PC19]) yields the hybrid transport dissimilarity:

\[ \delta _w(x, x_m) \eqdef \frac {d^2(x, x_m)}{\sqrt {\sigma (x)\,\sigma (x_m)}} + \bigl (\sigma (x) - \sigma (x_m)\bigr )^2. \]

The standalone functional interface (baux.enn_init, baux.with_geometry, baux.enn_fit, baux.enn_posterior, and baux.enn_sample) exposes these geometries directly. On a bimodal 6D design combining a tight low-noise basin with a coarse high-noise shell, Poincaré lifting isolates basin neighbors, separates epistemic (.se_epi) from heteroscedastic aleatoric (.se_ale) uncertainty, and produces spatially correlated draws across adjacent basin queries:

import numpy as np
import baux

rng = np.random.default_rng(7)
basin_x = 0.6 + 0.04 * rng.normal(size=(24, 6))
shell_x = rng.uniform(-1.5, 1.5, size=(24, 6))
train_x = np.vstack([basin_x, shell_x])

signal = np.exp(-1.5 * np.sum((train_x - 0.6) ** 2, axis=1, keepdims=True))
train_yvar = np.vstack([np.full((24, 1), 5e-4), np.full((24, 1), 5e-2)])
train_y = signal + np.sqrt(train_yvar) * rng.normal(size=(48, 1))

k_fit, k_draw = baux.split(baux.key(11), 2)
enn_state = baux.with_geometry(
    baux.enn_init(train_x, train_y, train_yvar, scale_x=True),
    "hyperbolic",
    local_k=4,
)
params = baux.enn_fit(enn_state, k=8, infer_aleatoric=False, key=k_fit)

queries = np.vstack([
    np.full((1, 6), 0.600),
    np.full((1, 6), 0.605),
    np.full((1, 6), 2.400),
])
post = baux.enn_posterior(
    enn_state, queries, params, flags=baux.enn.PosteriorFlags(observation_noise=True)
)
draws, idx = baux.enn_sample(enn_state, queries, params, key=k_draw, num_draws=64)

corr_basin = float(np.corrcoef(draws[0, 0], draws[1, 0])[0, 1])
assert np.all(idx[0] < 24)
assert post.se_epi[0, 0] < post.se_epi[2, 0]
assert np.allclose(post.se**2, post.se_epi**2 + post.se_ale**2)
assert corr_basin > 0.9
