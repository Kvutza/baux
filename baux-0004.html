BAUX Benchmark Playground

Interactive browser simulator and live run-telemetry explorer covering all seven BAUX benchmark families: Host Multi-TR Regions, Resident GPU/Metal Regions, Tokamak Plasma Control, Oceananigans.jl 3D Fluid, FUSE.jl Whole Facility & Dynamic, HardCaml RTL Synthesis, and Pretrain & Resident Program.

Select any benchmark family below to step the in-browser BAUX multi-trust-region optimizer (Latin hypercube initialization, \(K\)-NN ENN surrogate, UCB/Pareto acquisition) or inspect recorded ./baux tune CBOR telemetry from native Rust/Metal evaluators and zero-copy atomic ShmSlab warm Julia daemons (Oceananigans.jl 0.117.2 and FUSE.jl 1.2.1). When served locally via ./baux pages serve, live runs dispatch directly over /api/simulate.
