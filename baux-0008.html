Domain Charts & Codomain Operators

1. Section–Retraction Manifold Charts

Search spaces decompose as products \(\mathcal {M} = \prod _{\ell =1}^L \mathcal {M}_\ell \) of coordinate factors: positive intervals \([a, b] \subset \mathbb {R}_+\), periodic intervals \(\lbrack a, b\rparen \cong S^1\), probability simplices \(\Delta ^{d-1}\), and finite categorical sets. Each factor \(\mathcal {M}_\ell \) carries an embedding \(\psi _\ell : \mathcal {M}_\ell \to \mathcal {U}_\ell \subseteq \mathbb {R}^{d_\ell }\) and retraction \(\rho _\ell : \mathcal {U}_\ell \to \mathcal {M}_\ell \) satisfying the left-inverse identity \(\rho _\ell \circ \psi _\ell = \mathrm {id}_{\mathcal {M}_\ell }\) (deterministically for Log, Periodic, Simplex, and one-hot Categorical, and whenever Rademacher codewords are distinct):

\[ \begin {CD} \mathcal {M}_\ell @>{\psi _\ell }>> \mathcal {U}_\ell \\ @V{\mathrm {id}_{\mathcal {M}_\ell }}VV @VV{\rho _\ell }V \\ \mathcal {M}_\ell @= \mathcal {M}_\ell \end {CD} \]

Proposition 3.1 (Chart Metric Properties). The factor charts \((\psi _\ell , \rho _\ell )\) satisfy:

1. Multiplicative positive reals (Čencov [Cen82]): For \(0 < a < b < \infty \) on \([a, b]\) equipped with the scale-invariant metric \(ds^2 = (dx / x)^2\), the logarithmic chart

\[ \psi (x) \eqdef \frac {\log (x / a)}{\log (b / a)} \in [0, 1], \qquad \rho (u) \eqdef a \left (\frac {b}{a}\right )^{\Pi _{[0,1]}(u)}, \]

satisfies \(|\psi (x) - \psi (x')| = \frac {1}{\log (b/a)}|\log (x/x')|\), an exact isometry up to constant factor \(\log (b / a)\).

2. Periodic angular phases: For period \(P = b - a > 0\) on \(\lbrack a, b\rparen \cong S^1\) with phase \(\phi (x) \eqdef 2\pi (x - a)/P \in \lbrack 0, 2\pi \rparen \), the circular trigonometric embedding maps \(S^1\) onto the circle of radius \((2\pi )^{-1}\) centered at \((\tfrac {1}{2}, \tfrac {1}{2}) \in [0, 1]^2\):

\[ \begin {aligned} \psi (x) &\eqdef \left (\frac {1}{2} + \frac {\cos \phi (x)}{2\pi },\, \frac {1}{2} + \frac {\sin \phi (x)}{2\pi }\right ), \\ \rho (u_1, u_2) &\eqdef a + \frac {P}{2\pi }\Bigl (\operatorname {atan2}\bigl (u_2 - \tfrac {1}{2},\, u_1 - \tfrac {1}{2}\bigr ) \bmod 2\pi \Bigr ), \end {aligned} \]

with \(\rho \) smooth on \(\mathbb {R}^2 \setminus \{(\tfrac {1}{2}, \tfrac {1}{2})\}\). The embedded curve has unit arc-length circumference, and its Euclidean chord distance \(\Vert \psi (x) - \psi (x')\Vert _2 = \frac {1}{\pi }\bigl |\sin (\Delta \phi / 2)\bigr |\) is strictly increasing in shortest circular angle \(\Delta \phi \in [0, \pi ]\).

3. Probability simplex (Bhattacharyya [Bha43]): On \(\Delta ^{d-1} \eqdef \{p \in \mathbb {R}_+^d : \sum _{j=1}^d p_j = 1\}\) with Fisher–Rao metric \(g_{\mathrm {FR}} = \sum _{j=1}^d (dp_j)^2 / p_j\), the positive-orthant square-root map and clamped retraction (for \(\max (u, 0) \ne 0\))

\[ \psi (p) \eqdef \bigl (\sqrt {p_1}, \dots , \sqrt {p_d}\bigr ) \in S_+^{d-1}, \qquad \rho (u) \eqdef \frac {\max (u, 0)^{\odot 2}}{\Vert \max (u, 0)\Vert _2^2}, \]

satisfy \(\psi ^* g_{\mathbb {R}^d} = \frac {1}{4}\, g_{\mathrm {FR}}\) on the interior of \(\Delta ^{d-1}\), while squared chord distance gives the squared Hellinger distance:

\[ \Vert \psi (p) - \psi (q)\Vert _2^2 = \sum _{j=1}^d \bigl (\sqrt {p_j} - \sqrt {q_j}\bigr )^2 = 2 \left (1 - \sum _{j=1}^d \sqrt {p_j q_j}\right ). \]

4. Discrete categorical codebooks: For \(C\) categories, encoding="auto" embeds small sets (\(C \le 15\)) via scaled one-hot vectors in \(\mathbb {R}^C\) (pairwise distance \(\sqrt {2}\,s\) for \(c \ne c'\), \(s = 10\)), and larger sets into \(\mathbb {R}^{d_c}\) (\(d_c = \lceil 8 \ln C \rceil \)) via Rademacher codewords \(\psi (c) \in \{-s/\sqrt {d_c},\, +s/\sqrt {d_c}\}^{d_c}\) (Johnson & Lindenstrauss [JL84], Achlioptas [Ach03]), retracting by nearest codeword:

\[ \rho (u) \eqdef \arg \min _{c \in \{1, \dots , C\}} \Vert u - \psi (c)\Vert _2. \]

Proof. On \([a, b]\), \(d\psi = dx / (x \log (b/a))\), so \((d\psi )^2 = (dx / x)^2 / \log ^2(b/a)\). On \(\lbrack a, b\rparen \), \(\Vert \psi (x) - \psi (x')\Vert _2^2 = \frac {1}{4\pi ^2}\bigl ((\cos \phi - \cos \phi ')^2 + (\sin \phi - \sin \phi ')^2\bigr ) = \frac {1}{2\pi ^2}(1 - \cos \Delta \phi ) = \frac {1}{\pi ^2}\sin ^2(\Delta \phi / 2)\), which is strictly increasing for \(\Delta \phi \in [0, \pi ]\). On \(\mathrm {int}(\Delta ^{d-1})\), \(d\psi _j = dp_j / (2\sqrt {p_j})\), whence \(\sum _{j=1}^d (d\psi _j)^2 = \frac {1}{4}\sum _{j=1}^d (dp_j)^2 / p_j\), and expanding \(\sum _{j=1}^d (\sqrt {p_j} - \sqrt {q_j})^2\) with \(\sum _j p_j = \sum _j q_j = 1\) yields \(2(1 - \sum _j \sqrt {p_j q_j})\). Finally, since every one-hot or Rademacher codeword has constant Euclidean norm (\(\Vert \psi (c)\Vert _2^2 = s^2\)), \(\Vert u - \psi (c)\Vert _2^2 = \Vert u\Vert _2^2 + s^2 - 2\langle u, \psi (c)\rangle \), so nearest-neighbor retraction coincides with \(\arg \max _c \langle u, \psi (c)\rangle \) (breaking ties deterministically).\(\;\square \)

2. Codomain Shrinkage & Calibration

Given raw loading matrix \(L \in \mathbb {R}^{M \times r}\) (\(1 \le r < M\)) orthonormalized onto the Stiefel manifold \(V = L(L^\top L)^{-1/2} \in \mathrm {St}(r, M)\) via polar decomposition (Higham [Hig86]) with orthogonal complement \(V_\perp \in \mathrm {St}(M-r, M)\), the orthogonal isomorphism \(P \eqdef (V^\top ,\, V_\perp ^\top )^\top : \mathbb {R}^M \xrightarrow {\cong } \mathbb {R}^r \oplus \mathbb {R}^{M-r}\) factors codomain contraction (Stein [Ste56], James & Stein [JS61]):

\[ \begin {CD} \mathbb {R}^M @>{P}>> \mathbb {R}^r \oplus \mathbb {R}^{M-r} \\ @V{\hat {T}_{\alpha }}VV @VV{I_r \oplus \alpha I_{M-r}}V \\ \mathbb {R}^M @<<{P^\top }< \mathbb {R}^r \oplus \mathbb {R}^{M-r} \end {CD} \]

Theorem 3.2 (Oracle Linear-Bayes Subspace Dominance). Let \(y = \theta + \eta \in \mathbb {R}^M\) satisfy \(\mathbb {E}[\eta ] = 0\), and decompose \(y\) along \(V \in \mathrm {St}(r, M)\) (\(1 \le r < M\)) into \(z_\parallel \eqdef V^\top y \in \mathbb {R}^r\) with noise variance \(v_\parallel \eqdef \mathbb {E}\Vert V^\top \eta \Vert _2^2\) and normal coordinates \(z_\perp \eqdef V_\perp ^\top y \in \mathbb {R}^{M-r}\) with noise variance \(v_\perp \eqdef \mathbb {E}\Vert V_\perp ^\top \eta \Vert _2^2 > 0\) and squared orthogonal signal norm \(b_\perp ^2 \eqdef \Vert V_\perp ^\top \theta \Vert _2^2 \ge 0\). Over the linear class \(\hat {\theta }_\alpha \eqdef V z_\parallel + \alpha V_\perp z_\perp \) (\(\alpha \in \mathbb {R}\)), the oracle linear-Bayes weight \(\alpha ^* \eqdef b_\perp ^2 / (b_\perp ^2 + v_\perp )\) uniquely minimizes \(\mathcal {R}(\alpha ) \eqdef \mathbb {E}\Vert \hat {\theta }_\alpha - \theta \Vert _2^2\), achieving

\[ \mathcal {R}(\alpha ^*) = v_\parallel + \frac {v_\perp b_\perp ^2}{b_\perp ^2 + v_\perp } < \mathcal {R}(1) = v_\parallel + v_\perp . \]

Proof. By orthogonality of \(V\) and \(V_\perp \) and \(\mathbb {E}[z_\perp - V_\perp ^\top \theta ] = 0\),

\[ \mathcal {R}(\alpha ) = \mathbb {E}\Vert V^\top \eta \Vert _2^2 + \mathbb {E}\Vert \alpha V_\perp ^\top \eta - (1 - \alpha ) V_\perp ^\top \theta \Vert _2^2 = v_\parallel + \alpha ^2 v_\perp + (1 - \alpha )^2 b_\perp ^2. \]

Since \(v_\perp + b_\perp ^2 > 0\), \(\mathcal {R}(\alpha )\) is strictly convex with unique stationary point \(2\alpha v_\perp - 2(1 - \alpha )b_\perp ^2 = 0\), giving \(\alpha ^* = b_\perp ^2 / (b_\perp ^2 + v_\perp ) \in \lbrack 0, 1\rparen \). Substituting \(\alpha ^*\) gives

\[ \begin {aligned} \mathcal {R}(\alpha ^*) &= v_\parallel + \frac {b_\perp ^4 v_\perp + v_\perp ^2 b_\perp ^2}{(b_\perp ^2 + v_\perp )^2} = v_\parallel + \frac {v_\perp b_\perp ^2}{b_\perp ^2 + v_\perp }, \\[4pt] \mathcal {R}(1) - \mathcal {R}(\alpha ^*) &= v_\perp - \frac {v_\perp b_\perp ^2}{b_\perp ^2 + v_\perp } = \frac {v_\perp ^2}{b_\perp ^2 + v_\perp } > 0.\;\square \end {aligned} \]

Because \(b_\perp ^2\) is unknown in practice, finite-sample dominance requires estimating \(\alpha ^*\). Under isotropic Gaussian noise \(\eta \sim \mathcal {N}(0, \sigma ^2 I_M)\) whenever the normal codimension satisfies \(M - r \ge 3\), the positive-part James–Stein weight \(\hat {\alpha }_{\mathrm {JS}} = \bigl (1 - (M - r - 2)\sigma ^2 / \Vert z_\perp \Vert _2^2\bigr )_+\) uniformly dominates \(\alpha = 1\) in frequentist risk (James & Stein [JS61], Efron & Morris [EM73]); baux's online stein operator applies the moment-ratio plug-in \(\hat {\alpha } = \Vert z_\perp \Vert _2^2 / (\Vert z_\perp \Vert _2^2 + \hat {v}_\perp )\) across arbitrary codimension \(M - r \ge 1\).

Residual dispersion can be post-hoc rescaled via affine weighted least squares [Ait35] and split-conformal prediction (Papadopoulos et al. [Pap+02], Vovk et al. [VGS05], Lei et al. [LGR+18]). Given an independent held-out calibration split \(\mathcal {D}_{\mathrm {cal}} = \{(x_i, y_i)\}_{i=1}^{N_c}\) and miscoverage level \(\alpha \in \bigl \lbrack \frac {1}{N_c + 1},\, 1\bigr \rparen \), standardized scores \(E_i \eqdef |y_i - \hat {\mu }(x_i)| / \hat {\sigma }(x_i)\) define the order-statistic multiplier

\[ \begin {aligned} \hat {q}_{1-\alpha } &\eqdef E_{(\lceil (N_c + 1)(1 - \alpha )\rceil )}, \\ C_{1-\alpha }(x) &\eqdef \bigl [\hat {\mu }(x) - \hat {q}_{1-\alpha }\,\hat {\sigma }(x),\, \hat {\mu }(x) + \hat {q}_{1-\alpha }\,\hat {\sigma }(x)\bigr ]. \end {aligned} \]

(For \(\alpha < \frac {1}{N_c + 1}\), the implementation clamps the rank to \(N_c\), returning the empirical maximum residual; for alpha=None, it uses RMS residual scaling.) When \(\mathcal {D}_{\mathrm {cal}} \cup \{(x_{\mathrm {new}}, y_{\mathrm {new}})\}\) is exchangeable and disjoint from the surrogate training history, \(\mathbb {P}\bigl (y_{\mathrm {new}} \in C_{1-\alpha }(x_{\mathrm {new}})\bigr ) \ge 1 - \alpha \); on adaptive sequential optimization histories where exchangeability fails, \(\hat {q}_{1-\alpha }\) serves as an empirical scale heuristic rather than a distribution-free coverage guarantee.
