Optimizer::ask body LLBC does not. No Aeneas translation or Lean theorem for
the direct body was checked. None of these probes proves executable equivalence.
The generic declaration predicate context is semantic; silently dropping it
would weaken the extracted program. The experimental path was not integrated.

Bounded alternative: Charon's FnPtrKind::Fun points to FunDeclId and FunDecl
supports Body::Opaque. An occurrence-specific external contract could register
a synthetic FunDecl from the rustc-typechecked callsite's instantiated fn_sig,
stable DefId, generic arguments and late-bound binder. Charon has no current
API for that: translate_bound_fn_ptr calls translate_item_maybe_enqueue, then
translate_item_aux builds Hax full_def for visible items. A new registration
path, stable identity policy and contract provenance would be required. Such
a contract remains a proof obligation for ndarray behavior; it is not a proof
of the external dependency.

The local production comparison must target Python-visible natural-coordinate
ask/tell behavior: unit-to-natural conversion in ask and natural-to-unit
conversion in tell.
