Control & Physics Simulation

The viewer above renders 3D cutaway vessel and coil geometries, equilibrium flux surfaces, stratified fluid fields, and closed-loop policy trajectories exported from FUSE.jl 1.2.1 (Meneghini et al. [Men+24]) and Oceananigans.jl 0.117.2 (Ramadhan et al. [Ram+20]); see the Overview for the ENN surrogate and multi-region search formulation.

1. Exported Tokamak & Fluid Geometries

The 3D cutaway viewport and paired 2D cross-section load device geometries and PDE states exported from two Julia solver suites:

  • Tokamak vessels, coils, and equilibria (FUSE.jl 1.2.1): For SPARC, ARC, ITER, and DIII-D (alongside the 25-vertex TCV vessel and 20 E/F/OH coils), evals/fuse/worker.jl initializes IMAS.dd() device models via FUSE.init, solves Grad–Shafranov equilibria and active coil currents with ActorEquilibrium(:TEQUILA), ActorPFdesign, and ActorPFactive, and exports vacuum vessel shells, first-wall contours, toroidal-field TF D-coils, central solenoid CS/OH stacks, poloidal-field PF rings, and last-closed-flux-surface (LCFS) contours together with \(I_p\), \(B_0\), \(q_{95}\), \(\beta _N\), and \(P_{\mathrm {fus}}\).
  • Stratified shear and wind-mixing tanks (Oceananigans.jl 0.117.2): The Kelvin–Helmholtz Shear Tank and Wind-Mixing Basin views display channel boundaries, actuator vanes, 3D isopycnal surfaces, and velocity streamlines exported by evals/oceananigans/worker.jl (NonhydrostaticModel \(Ri = 0.1\) shear-billow suppression with 5th-order upwind advection and RungeKutta3, and rotating FPlane HydrostaticFreeSurfaceModel mixed-layer deepening).

2. TCV Rampdown Policy Search (toka_gym)

On the open-source EPFL/MIT toka_gym TCV plasma rampdown benchmark (Wang et al. [Wan+25], Theiler et al. [The+26]), a four-layer policy \(\pi _\theta : \mathbb {R}^{11} \to [-1, 1]^8\) of hidden width \(256\) (\(D = 136{,}712\) weights) is searched directly on closed-loop trajectories (\(20\) steps \(\times \, 4\) substeps) over \(10{,}000\) sequential rounds across \(n = 10\) common-random-number seeds (\(800{,}000\) rollouts across \(8\) configurations; initial error 58.12 ± 31.39, exact paired initialization drift 0.00). Multi-region surrogate-free TuRBO-zero (\(R = 10\), sparse coordinate perturbations of expected block size \(d_p = 13{,}671\)) produces identical trajectories across all four cross-region sharing topologies (origin-only, box-contain, ellipsoid-ball, nearest-center), dropping below on-policy PPO (20.59) by round \(2\) (20.33 ± 6.58) and reaching 1.42 ± 0.03 (1.4192 ± 0.0306) at round \(250\), 1.38 ± 0.04 at round \(1{,}000\), 1.36 ± 0.03 at round \(3{,}000\), and 1.31 ± 0.02 (1.3112 ± 0.0157) at round \(10{,}000\).

Single-region TuRBO-ENN (\(R = 1\), \(K = 10\) Epistemic Nearest Neighbors fitted by leave-one-out log-likelihood with UCB acquisition; baux.enn) trails \(R = 10\) at round \(250\) (1.58 ± 0.05 hyperbolic to 1.68 ± 0.08 euclidean; wasserstein 1.68 ± 0.06, exact sign-flip \(p_{\mathrm {Holm}} = 0.014\)) and steadily closes the gap over \(10{,}000\) rounds, reaching 1.34 ± 0.03 (1.3409 ± 0.0301, wasserstein, paired difference +0.03 ± 0.04, \(p_{\mathrm {Holm}} = 1.00\); 1.35 ± 0.02 euclidean; 1.35 ± 0.03 hyperbolic; 1.37 ± 0.03 conformal).

3. TORAX Transport & Shaping Policy Search

To study cross-region incumbent sharing over \(10{,}000\) rounds on an open-source transport solver, we evaluate a \(93 \to 256 \to 256 \to 256 \to 19\) ELU+\(\tanh \) policy (\(D = 160{,}531\) parameters; policy architecture following Degrave et al. [Deg+22], Tracey et al. [Tra+24], and Pfau et al. [Pfa+25]) in TORAX 2.0.0 (Citrin et al. [Cit+24]). Each rollout evolves iterhybrid geometry (\(R_0 = 6.2\text { m}\), \(B_0 = 5.3\text { T}\), \(15\) warmup steps followed by \(T = 20\) controlled steps) coupled to four 1D radial transport PDEs for \(T_e(\hat {\rho })\), \(T_i(\hat {\rho })\), \(n_e(\hat {\rho })\), and \(\psi (\hat {\rho })\) over \(\hat {\rho } \in [0, 1]\), mapping \(93\) radial profile and shaping residuals to \(19\) normalized plasma-current, heating, current-drive, fueling, and Miller local-equilibrium shaping (kappa, delta_u, delta_l, \(Z_{\mathrm {axis}}\); Miller et al. [Mil+98]) actuators:

\[ \begin {aligned} R(\hat {\rho }, \vartheta ) &= R_{\mathrm {geo}}(\hat {\rho }) + \hat {\rho }\,a_{\min }\cos \bigl (\vartheta + \arcsin (\delta (\hat {\rho },\vartheta ))\sin \vartheta \bigr ), \\[4pt] Z(\hat {\rho }, \vartheta ) &= Z_{\mathrm {axis}} + \kappa (\hat {\rho })\,\hat {\rho }\,a_{\min }\sin \vartheta . \end {aligned} \]

Following the multi-component reward formulation of Pfau et al. [Pfa+25] (Table 6, Eq. 31), five normalized tracking and actuator-regularization scores—Miller contour error against the 32-point shape_70166 template, plasma current tracking \(I_p\), shaping-effort margin, actuator bounds compliance, and auxiliary balance—pass through target/bad-threshold sigmoids \(c_m(v)\) (\(\zeta = -\ln 19\)) and are combined over \(T = 20\) steps on \([0, 100]\) via SmoothMax (\(\alpha = -3.0\)):

\[ \begin {aligned} c_m(v) &= \operatorname {clip}\left (2\,\sigma \left (\zeta \,\frac {v - g_m}{b_m - g_m}\right ),\, 0,\, 1\right ), \\[4pt] \mathcal {J}(\theta ) &= \frac {100}{T}\sum _{t=1}^{T} \operatorname {SmoothMax}_{\alpha = -3}\bigl (c_{\mathrm {LCFS},t},\, c_{I_p,t},\, c_{\mathrm {PF},t},\, c_{\mathrm {volt},t},\, c_{\mathrm {OH},t}\bigr ), \\[4pt] \operatorname {SmoothMax}_{\alpha }(x_1,\dots ,x_5) &= \frac {\sum _{m=1}^{5} x_m e^{\alpha x_m}}{\sum _{m=1}^{5} e^{\alpha x_m}}. \end {aligned} \]

Both configurations below run surrogate-free multi-region TuRBO-zero (acquisition = "random", \(R = 10\) regions, incumbent-ucb1 routing, sparse coordinate perturbations of expected block size \(d_p = 16{,}053\), \(2\) candidates and \(1\) arm per round; baux.regions) across \(n = 10\) paired seeds so only cross-region incumbent sharing differs. Entries report sample mean ± SEM (ddof = 1); unadjusted two-sided paired \(t_9\) \(p\)-values are listed at rounds 8,000 and 10,000, Holm–Bonferroni adjustment across the \(6\) post-initialization checkpoints gives \(p_{\mathrm {Holm}} = 0.246\), and an on-policy PPO run reaches 44.25 at round 10,000.
