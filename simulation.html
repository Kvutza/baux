Tokamak & 3D Physics CAD Simulation

The interactive 3D WebGL cutaway CAD simulation above unifies closed-loop tokamak magnetic/transport control and 3D geophysical fluid control across environments built directly by FUSE.jl 1.2.1 (Meneghini et al. [Men+24]) and Oceananigans.jl 0.117.2 (Ramadhan et al. [Ram+20]) over BAUX's page-aligned ShmSlab shared-memory layer; see also the Overview for the local ENN surrogate and multi-region portfolio formulation.

1. FUSE.jl & Oceananigans.jl 3D CAD Environment Builders

Rather than relying on synthetic toy contours, the central 3D CAD viewport and paired 2D physical cross-sections hydrate directly from persistent Julia solver environments:

  • Tokamak CAD & TEQUILA Equilibrium (FUSE.jl 1.2.1): For SPARC, ARC, ITER, and DIII-D (alongside EPFL's 25-vertex TCV vessel and 20 E/F/OH coils), evals/fuse/worker.jl initializes full IMAS.dd() device descriptions via FUSE.init, solves free/fixed-boundary Grad–Shafranov equilibria and active coil currents with ActorEquilibrium(:TEQUILA), ActorPFdesign, and ActorPFactive, and exports closed radial-build 3D CAD shells (double-walled vacuum vessel, first wall/divertor limiter, discrete superconducting toroidal-field TF D-coils, central solenoid CS/OH stacks, and PF shaping rings) together with solved LCFS contours, magnetic axes, \(I_p\), \(B_0\), \(q_{95}\), \(\beta _N\), and \(P_{\mathrm {fus}}\).
  • 3D Stratified Shear & Ocean Basin CAD (Oceananigans.jl 0.117.2): Selecting the Kelvin–Helmholtz Shear Tank or Wind-Mixing Basin in the environment selector switches the 3D CAD viewport to the structural containment channel, actuator manifolds/surface flux vanes, 3D Phong-lit overturning isopycnal material surfaces, and advected 3D velocity streamlines exported by evals/oceananigans/worker.jl (NonhydrostaticModel \(Ri = 0.1\) billow suppression with 5th-order upwind advection and RungeKutta3, and rotating FPlane HydrostaticFreeSurfaceModel mixed-layer deepening).

2. TokaGym Rampdown Control with ENN-UCB

On the open-source EPFL/MIT toka_gym TCV rampdown benchmark (Wang et al. [Wan+25], Theiler et al. [The+26]), a four-layer policy \(\pi _\theta : \mathbb {R}^{11} \to [-1, 1]^8\) of hidden width \(256\) (\(D = 136{,}712\) parameters) is searched directly from closed-loop trajectories (\(20\) steps \(\times \, 4\) substeps). Multi-region ENN-TuRBO fits a \(K = 10\) Epistemic Nearest Neighbor surrogate by LOO pseudolikelihood with UCB acquisition across \(R = 10\) regions (incumbent-ucb1 routing, coordinate-subspace perturbations of expected block size \(d_p = 13{,}671\), \(4\) candidates and \(1\) evaluated arm per round). Across \(n = 10\) paired seeds over \(100\) rounds, tracking error falls from 65.72 at initialization to 1.79 (ellipsoid-ball sharing) and 1.68 (origin-only) versus 20.59 for on-policy Metal PPO at matching round budget.

3. TORAX Core-Transport & Shaping Regional Ablation

Because EPFL's free-boundary Grad–Shafranov coil-circuit simulator FGE used in Degrave et al. [Deg+22], Tracey et al. [Tra+24], and Pfau et al. [Pfa+25] is proprietary, we evaluate the \(93 \to 256 \to 256 \to 256 \to 19\) ELU+\(\tanh \) policy architecture (\(D = 160{,}531\) parameters) in open-source TORAX 2.0.0 (Citrin et al. [Cit+24]). Rather than solving free-boundary E/F/OH/FPS coil circuits from \(38\) magnetic probes and \(34\) flux loops, this benchmark evolves iterhybrid CHEASE/MEQ geometry (\(R_0 = 6.2\text { m}\), \(B_0 = 5.3\text { T}\), \(15\) warmup steps followed by \(T = 20\) controlled steps) coupled to four 1D radial transport PDEs for \(T_e(\hat {\rho })\), \(T_i(\hat {\rho })\), \(n_e(\hat {\rho })\), and \(\psi (\hat {\rho })\) over \(\hat {\rho } \in [0, 1]\), mapping \(93\) radial profile and shaping residuals to \(19\) normalized plasma current, heating, current-drive, pellet/gas fueling, and Miller local-equilibrium shaping (kappa, delta_u, delta_l, \(Z_{\mathrm {axis}}\); Miller et al. [Mil+98]) actuators:

\[ \begin {aligned} R(\hat {\rho }, \vartheta ) &= R_{\mathrm {geo}}(\hat {\rho }) + \hat {\rho }\,a_{\min }\cos \bigl (\vartheta + \arcsin (\delta (\hat {\rho },\vartheta ))\sin \vartheta \bigr ), \\[4pt] Z(\hat {\rho }, \vartheta ) &= Z_{\mathrm {axis}} + \kappa (\hat {\rho })\,\hat {\rho }\,a_{\min }\sin \vartheta . \end {aligned} \]

Adapting the multi-component reward structure of Pfau et al. [Pfa+25] (Table 6, Eq. 31), five normalized tracking and actuator-regularization scores—normalized Miller contour error against the 32-point shape_70166 aspect template, plasma current tracking \(I_p\), shaping-effort margin, actuator bounds compliance, and auxiliary balance—are mapped through target/bad-threshold sigmoids \(c_m(v)\) (\(\zeta = -\ln 19\)) and averaged over \(T = 20\) steps on a \([0, 100]\) scale via SmoothMax (\(\alpha = -3.0\)):

\[ \begin {aligned} c_m(v) &= \operatorname {clip}\left (2\,\sigma \left (\zeta \,\frac {v - g_m}{b_m - g_m}\right ),\, 0,\, 1\right ), \\[4pt] \mathcal {J}(\theta ) &= \frac {100}{T}\sum _{t=1}^{T} \operatorname {SmoothMax}_{\alpha = -3}\bigl (c_{\mathrm {LCFS},t},\, c_{I_p,t},\, c_{\mathrm {PF},t},\, c_{\mathrm {volt},t},\, c_{\mathrm {OH},t}\bigr ), \\[4pt] \operatorname {SmoothMax}_{\alpha }(x_1,\dots ,x_5) &= \frac {\sum _{m=1}^{5} x_m e^{\alpha x_m}}{\sum _{m=1}^{5} e^{\alpha x_m}}. \end {aligned} \]

To isolate cross-region incumbent adoption (baux.regions) over \(10{,}000\) sequential evaluations without surrogate fitting variance, both configurations below run surrogate-free multi-region TuRBO (acquisition = "random", i.e. TuRBO-zero, with \(R = 10\) regions, incumbent-ucb1 routing, subspace perturbations of expected block size \(d_p = 16{,}053\), \(2\) candidates and \(1\) arm per round) across \(n = 10\) paired random seeds (entries report sample mean ± SEM with ddof = 1; unadjusted two-sided paired \(t_9\) \(p\)-values are shown at rounds 8,000 and 10,000, while Holm–Bonferroni adjustment across the \(6\) post-init checkpoints gives \(p_{\mathrm {Holm}} = 0.246\); an on-policy PPO baseline reaches 44.25 at round 10,000).
